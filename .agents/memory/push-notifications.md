---
name: Push notifications
description: Operational constraints and delivery modes for Petrotrack notifications
---

Petrotrack notifications have two delivery paths: a server-backed recipient inbox with foreground polling, and Expo push delivery for background or closed-app alerts.

**Why:** Web previews and Expo Go cannot fully exercise native push registration, while the app must still preserve alerts reliably when a device is offline or the app is closed.

Pump-report workflow events and server-scheduled My Folder document-expiry reminders may create a notification inbox row, native push, badge, or sound. Chats—including direct, group, media, calls, and operational chat alerts—remain solely in the Chats experience.

**Why:** Staff need a reliable operational notification channel without chat traffic obscuring approvals, report updates, or expiring compliance documents.

**How to apply:** Treat the database inbox as authoritative. Document expiry reminders are generated by the server rather than a mobile client, preserving delivery even when nobody has the app open. Verify actual background delivery only in an EAS-built iOS/Android app with notification permissions granted; keep web/simulator checks focused on inboxes, badges, and foreground sound.
