---
name: Net-profit finance costs
description: Accounting treatment and edit authority for bank charges and interest paid on loans.
---

Bank charges and interest paid on loans are non-negative accountant-maintained monthly finance costs. Deduct both, along with asset depreciation, from operating profit before calculating CITA and net profit.

**Why:** These costs reduce taxable profit and must not be omitted from Accounts, Financial Status, P&L reports, or report exports.

**How to apply:** Use the shared net-profit calculation for every displayed or exported P&L path. Preserve zero defaults for older saved financial entries, and keep the edit controls in the accountant-maintained Financial Entries flow.
