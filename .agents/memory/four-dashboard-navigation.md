---
name: Primary dashboard navigation
description: The Petrotrack mobile app uses five primary destinations with operational modules grouped beneath My Jobs.
---

The primary navigation uses Home, My Jobs, My Messages, My Folder, and My Profile. My Folder is a dashboard-level destination for the approved corporate records areas, not an in-page shortcut. It is visible to the Accountant, Marketing Manager, Corporate Services Manager/HR, MD, and CEO. Home is the shift-handoff workspace and overview surface; displayed operational values should link to their underlying source screens for deeper scrutiny. Home handover notes are shared state, not device-only drafts. My Jobs is the work hub and groups Operations, Sales, Finance, Reports, and supporting business modules. Inventory and Maintenance are separate Operations destinations; Inventory should not embed a duplicate maintenance workflow. Tank Dipping owns Dip Form, Awaiting Approval, and approved-dipping History; Inventory owns current-stock and inventory analysis. Sales has one dedicated dashboard under My Jobs and should not be duplicated in Operations or Home. Existing module routes remain focused detail screens reached from My Jobs.

**Why:** Users clarified that My Folder is a peer dashboard for easy navigation, while sensitive folder contents still need role-based visibility.

**How to apply:** Add new operational work as a My Jobs section/card or a focused detail route; add a new primary destination only when it is a true dashboard-level workspace like My Folder; keep Home checkpoints derived from synchronized dips, deliveries, maintenance, and messages; save handover notes through shared app state; make Home metrics, alerts, summaries, and audit items tappable to their source; keep Tank Dipping focused on readings, approval status, and dipping history; keep Inventory focused on current stock and inventory analysis; keep Maintenance focused on maintenance requests; and keep Sales metrics/shift reporting in the dedicated Sales route.
