---
name: Operating capital formula
description: Business rule for operating capital and monthly start-up capital rollover
---

Operating capital is calculated as the month’s opening start-up capital plus net profit for that month. It is not a balance-sheet aggregation of stock, cash, receivables, loans, liabilities, or capital injections.

**Why:** This is the agreed accounting definition for the Accounts capital view and makes the figure traceable from one month to the next.

**How to apply:** Admins may seed the current month’s start-up capital. Store the resulting closing operating capital and use it as the next month’s opening amount when the month rolls over. Keep the formula display limited to these two inputs.
